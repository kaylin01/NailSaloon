Our Story

Pretty nails,
done properly.

"We don't rush a set. Every nail leaves the chair the way we'd want our own."

Polish Me Pretty Gang started with a simple belief: getting your nails done should feel like a treat, not a production line. So we built a calm, private studio in the heart of Morningside where you're the only client in the room — no queues, no rushing, just beautiful work and good company.

From sculpted acrylic tips to glossy gel overlays, fills, chrome and hand-painted art, every service uses salon-grade products that wear longer and stay kind to your natural nail. Tools are sterilised between every client, and files are single-use — because your health matters as much as your finish.
