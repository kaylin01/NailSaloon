How It Began

From one chair
to a six-week waitlist.

"I never wanted a busy salon. I wanted a quiet one — where every set is done properly."

Luxe Nails Studio began in 2021 as a single chair in Zanele's apartment: three loyal clients, one ring light, and a refusal to rush. Six months later, she signed the lease on a private studio in Sandton. Within a year, she was booked out a month ahead.

What sets the work apart isn't speed — it's restraint. Zanele won't take on a design she hasn't perfected, won't compromise on the products she uses, and won't squeeze three clients into the time one deserves. After her diploma in advanced nail technology in 2020, she trained under master artists in Cape Town and London to deepen her gel-art and structural acrylic skills.
